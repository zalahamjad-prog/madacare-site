Patient rights

At Mada Care we care for you like family, and we respect the rights of every patient we serve at home. These are your rights, and your family's, with us.

  1. 1

    Receiving treatment and care

    The patient must receive the treatment and care needed to recover or maintain their health, regardless of race, religion, sex, marital status, origin, age, disability, or how the treatment is paid for.

  2. 2

    Respect and privacy

    The patient has the right to be treated with respect and to have their privacy and dignity protected. Visual and auditory privacy must be provided during personal activities such as hygiene and clinical procedures.

  3. 3

    Care from qualified providers

    The patient must receive care from qualified healthcare providers with the education and experience needed to provide the service competently.

  4. 4

    Infection control

    Healthcare providers must follow the infection control rules set by the Ministry of Health to protect patients from infection.

  5. 5

    Respecting values and protecting rights

    Healthcare providers must respect patients' values and beliefs, protect their rights, and report any case of physical or psychological abuse.

  6. 6

    Appointing a legal guardian

    If the patient can't make decisions about their treatment, a legal guardian must be appointed to sign on their behalf, and the guardian's identity must be verified.

  7. 7

    Taking part in your care

    The patient and their family have the right to:

    • Talk to the care team to get complete, up-to-date information about the diagnosis, the treatment, its benefits and the expected results.
    • Know the medical alternatives, the risks and the treatment's success rate.
    • Know what care is available and the name of the doctor in charge.
    • Refuse treatment or stop it in line with the applicable regulations, after being told of their responsibilities and the alternatives.
    • Ask about any part of the treatment or the service provided.
  8. 8

    A safe environment

    A safe environment must be provided during treatment sessions, and the patient must be protected from any sexual or verbal abuse, neglect or mistreatment.

  9. 9

    Treatment cost information

    The patient must be told the cost of treatment in advance, including an explanation of the invoice and the services provided.

  10. 10

    Refusing to sign

    The patient has the right to refuse to sign any document that hasn't been explained to them clearly.

  11. 11

    Making a complaint

    The patient can tell the customer experience staff about any complaint or problem with their care, rights or safety.

Have a complaint or a question?

Our customer experience team will listen and follow up with you. Every complaint is handled confidentially.
